PHQ-9 Provider Protocol

Contemporary Health Center

Clinical action guide by PHQ-9 severity level — For provider use only

⚠ Question 9 Safety Protocol: If a patient endorses any score ≥1 on Question 9 (self-harm/suicidal ideation), regardless of total score:
  • Conduct immediate safety assessment — ask about plan, intent, means, and timeline
  • Determine if patient is safe to leave the clinic; if not, do not leave patient alone
  • Consider Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) for further assessment
  • Document safety plan, emergency contacts, and 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
  • If imminent risk: call 911 or escort to nearest ED
0 – 4 Minimal Depression
Clinical Action
  • No treatment typically indicated
  • Supportive education on mood wellness
  • Encourage healthy sleep, exercise, and social connection
Follow-Up
  • Re-screen at next routine visit or annually
  • Educate patient on when to seek help if symptoms change
5 – 9 Mild Depression
Clinical Action
  • Watchful waiting with active monitoring
  • Discuss lifestyle interventions: exercise, sleep hygiene, stress reduction
  • Consider brief counseling or psychoeducation
  • Evaluate contributing factors (medical, social, situational)
Follow-Up
  • Repeat PHQ-9 in 4 weeks
  • If no improvement after 4–8 weeks, consider escalation to moderate protocol
  • Provide behavioral health referral information
10 – 14 Moderate Depression
Clinical Action
  • Develop treatment plan with patient
  • Consider starting SSRI/SNRI if not contraindicated
  • Refer to therapy (CBT, behavioral activation preferred)
  • Screen for co-occurring anxiety, substance use, or medical causes
  • Rule out bipolar disorder before starting antidepressant
Follow-Up
  • Repeat PHQ-9 in 2–4 weeks
  • If medication started: assess tolerability at 1–2 weeks
  • Titrate medication if partial response at 4–6 weeks
  • Confirm therapy engagement
15 – 19 Moderately Severe Depression
Clinical Action
  • Initiate or optimize pharmacotherapy (SSRI/SNRI first line)
  • Strongly recommend therapy — combined treatment preferred
  • Complete safety screening (Question 9 + direct inquiry)
  • Assess functional impairment (work, relationships, ADLs)
  • Consider psychiatric consultation if complex presentation
Follow-Up
  • Repeat PHQ-9 in 1–2 weeks
  • Close follow-up until stable improvement
  • Reassess medication efficacy at 4–6 weeks; adjust or augment if needed
  • Document safety plan in chart
20 – 27 Severe Depression
Clinical Action
  • Immediate safety assessment required
  • Initiate pharmacotherapy if not already on medication
  • Urgent psychiatric referral — consider same-day if possible
  • Combined medication + therapy is standard of care
  • Evaluate need for higher level of care (IOP, PHP, inpatient)
  • Involve support person/family if appropriate and consented
Follow-Up
  • Repeat PHQ-9 in 1 week
  • Weekly contact (visit or phone) until score < 15
  • Verify psychiatric and therapy appointments scheduled
  • Create and document written safety plan
  • Provide crisis resources: 988, crisis text (text HOME to 741741)

Documentation Requirements (All Levels)

  • Record PHQ-9 total score and Question 9 response in chart
  • Document clinical decision-making, treatment plan, and patient agreement
  • Note any safety assessment performed and outcome
  • Include follow-up timeline and responsible provider
  • If medication prescribed: document rationale, starting dose, expected timeline for effect, and side effects discussed
