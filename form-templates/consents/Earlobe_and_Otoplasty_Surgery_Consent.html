INFORMED CONSENT — EARLOBE & OTOPLASTY SURGERY

GENERAL INFORMATION

Otoplasty is a surgical process to reshape the ear. Earlobe surgery is usually performed to address an enlarged, torn, or stretched earlobe. A variety of different techniques and approaches may be used to reshape congenital or patient induced prominence in the ears or to restore damaged ears.

ALTERNATIVE TREATMENTS

Earlobe surgery & otoplasty is usually an elective surgical operation. Alternative forms of management consist of not undergoing the otoplasty operation.

RISKS OF OTOPLASTY SURGERY

  • Bleeding: Possible though unusual bleeding during or after surgery.
  • Infection: Unusual after surgery; may require antibiotics or additional surgery.
  • Change in Skin Sensation: Diminished or loss of skin sensation in surgical areas.
  • Ear Trauma: Physical injury after procedure would disrupt results.
  • Skin Contour Irregularities: Contour irregularities and depressions may occur.
  • Scarring: Abnormal scars may occur; may require revision or treatment.
  • Surgical Anesthesia: Both local and general anesthesia involve risk.
  • Asymmetry: Normal differences between ears may result in variation.
  • Delayed Healing: Wound disruption or delayed wound healing is possible.
  • Allergic Reactions: Rare local allergies to tape, suture materials, etc.
  • Pain: Pain of varying intensity and duration may occur.
  • Damage to Deeper Structures: Potential injury to nerves, blood vessels, muscles.
  • Sutures: Deep non-absorbable sutures may become visible or produce irritation.
  • Skin Discoloration/Swelling: Bruising and swelling normally occur.
