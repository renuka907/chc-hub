PAYMENT AND APPOINTMENT POLICY

Thank you for choosing Contemporary Health Center to provide this service for you. We are proud to offer you the best treatment, skill and service at the best price. We are committed to keeping our prices low for our patients.

Unfortunately, missed appointments are costly and affect our ability to provide service to others. A missed appointment is a loss to three (3) people: the client who missed the valuable time, another client who could have used the valuable time, and the practitioner who was staffed and prepared for the treatment.

Please provide at least 48 business hours' notice to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Payment Policy

For your treatment, time and medical practitioners will be reserved during your appointment time.

Rescheduling and Cancellation Policy

Cancellation of reserved time made in under 48 business hours prior to your reserved appointment time or missed appointments will be charged a missed appointment fee.

Prepayment is required to schedule the procedure for those who are self-pay and are not applying health insurance coverage. Health insurance coverage will be verified before the appointment is set and any deductible or copay will be due.

  • 1st time late cancellation or missed appointment fee is $150. This fee must be paid to schedule another appointment. Insurance will NOT cover this fee.
  • 2nd time late cancellation or missed appointment fee is $500. This fee must be paid to schedule another appointment. Insurance will NOT cover this fee.

We will not reschedule or perform the procedure after you have missed or cancelled late 2 times. We will refer you to another practice and send your records wherever you need.

Transportation issues, medical illness, other medical treatments, and childcare issues do not qualify as exclusions to the policy.
