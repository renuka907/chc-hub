JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC Informed Consent

1. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

What is it?
JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC injectable gel is a smooth, clear, colorless hyaluronic acid (HA) gel that contains a small quantity of local anesthetic (lidocaine). HA is a naturally occurring sugar found in the human body. The role of HA in the skin is to help retain natural moisture and softness. The lidocaine helps to improve the comfort of the injection.

JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC injectable gel is manufactured using VYCROSS® technology to give a specialized smooth-gel filler that produces long-lasting results for up to 18 months at the treatment site.

What is it for?
JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC is injected with an ultrafine needle into areas of facial tissue where moderate to severe facial wrinkles and folds (such as nasolabial folds) occur. As you age, wrinkles develop on your face, and nasolabial folds may become more visible.

JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC is designed to temporarily reverse signs of aging by adding subtle volume to facial wrinkles and folds (such as nasolabial folds) and restoring a smoother appearance to the face in patients over the age of 21.

2. CONTRAINDICATIONS

You should not use JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC if:

  • You have severe allergies with a history of severe reactions (anaphylaxis) or history or presence of multiple severe allergies.
  • You are allergic to lidocaine or to the proteins used to make the HA in JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC (Gram-positive bacterial proteins).
  • You are planning laser treatment, chemical peeling, or any other procedure after treatment.
  • You are on therapy used to decrease the body's immune response.
  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • You have a history of excessive scarring (thick, hard scars).
  • You have a history of pigmentation disorders.

3. PRECAUTIONS

  • Avoid strenuous exercise, exposure to extensive sun or heat, and consumption of alcoholic beverages within the first 24 hours following treatment.
  • Tell your practitioner if you are using any medication that can prolong bleeding, such as aspirin, ibuprofen, or other blood thinners.

4. CLINICAL STUDY

To establish the safety and effectiveness of JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC, 123 subjects were treated. The amount used ranged from 0.1 mL to 3.0 mL per NLF, with a median volume of 1.7 mL.

5. BENEFITS

  • 93% of subjects had a 1-point-or-greater improvement in NLF severity 6 months after treatment
  • 59% of subjects had a 1-point-or-greater improvement at 18 months
  • Approximately 82% of subjects were highly satisfied at 6 months
  • Approximately 68% were highly satisfied at 18 months

6. RISKS

A majority of subjects (95%) experienced side effects such as firmness, swelling, tenderness, lumps/bumps, redness, pain, bruising, itching, and discoloration at the injection site. These were usually mild or moderate and went away within 1 week.

Serious risks include unintentional injection into a blood vessel which can cause vision abnormalities, blindness, stroke, temporary scabs, or permanent scarring.

7. AFTER PROCEDURE INFORMATION

Within the first 24 hours, avoid strenuous exercise, extensive sun or heat exposure, and alcoholic beverages. Makeup may be applied 1 hour after treatment. Results lasted for 18 months in a majority (59%) of subjects.

8. WHEN TO CALL YOUR PRACTITIONER

Call immediately if you have: changes in vision, signs of a stroke, white appearance of the skin, or unusual pain during or shortly after treatment.
