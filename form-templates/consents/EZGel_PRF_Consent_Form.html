PRF - PLATELET-RICH FIBRIN GEL CONSENT FORM

INSTRUCTIONS: This is an informed consent document that has been prepared to help inform you about the Platelet Rich Fibrin Gel technique. Please initial each section and sign the consent for the procedure.

INTRODUCTION

The Platelet-Rich Fibrin Gel technique uses the latest generation of Smart Blood Concentrates to generate a complex Fibrin Matrix rich in Platelets, Leukocytes and Mesenchymal stem cells. For 10-12 days this results in the constant release of Growth factors and interleukins which accelerate tissue healing and regenerating processes.

PRF Gel is effective in most cases; no guarantees can be made that a specific patient will benefit. The healing process takes time and the final result won't be readily visible for many months.

INDICATIONS OF USE

PRF technique is used to accelerate tissue healing and skin regeneration processes through Blood Concentrate. This proposed use is "Off-label" — not specifically approved by the FDA. It is not experimental and is safe and effective.

POSSIBLE RISKS AND SIDE EFFECTS

  1. DISCOMFORT: Discomfort may be experienced during blood draw and during injection.
  2. BRUISING, SWELLING, INFECTION: Bruising and swelling of the treated area may occur. Skin infection is rare but possible.
  3. SCARRING: Scar at entry point is extremely rare but must be considered.
  4. CONTRAINDICATIONS: Smokers may have less response. Cell death or Fibrosis may occur.

TREATMENT

  • You may take pain medication such as Tylenol.
  • A numbing cream is applied to the area of treatment.
  • Approximately 10 cc of whole blood is drawn from your arm.
  • The tubes of blood are centrifuged to separate the component cells.
  • The liquid is then transferred into a syringe and injected using a tiny needle.
