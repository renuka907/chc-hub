INFORMED CONSENT FOR KYBELLA® (DEOXYCHOLIC ACID)
AESTHETIC INJECTABLE TREATMENT

INTRODUCTION

Kybella® is a cytolytic drug indicated for improvement in the appearance of moderate to severe convexity or fullness associated with submental (neck) fat in adults. Kybella® is injected into the fat under the chin (no more than 50 injections or 10mL under the skin). Injections will be given at least one month apart.

ALTERNATIVE TREATMENTS

Non-surgical: topical neck products, weight loss, neck homeopathic treatments.
Surgical: neck lift, neck liposuction, and others.

RISKS

  • Irritations: Tingling, swelling, itching, skin tightness, and headache.
  • Nerve Injury: Could cause nerve injury resulting in uneven smile or facial muscle weakness (typically resolves in ~6 weeks).
  • Swallowing: Can temporarily cause trouble with swallowing.
  • Skin Ulceration: Could cause superficial skin erosions.
  • Alopecia: Could cause small patches of hair loss in the treatment area.
  • Bleeding: Possible bleeding during or after injections.
  • Blindness: Extremely rare; aesthetic injectables can cause permanent blindness by blocking blood supply to the eye.
  • Infection: Should not be injected if there is a preexisting infection in the treatment area.
  • Bruising and Swelling: Commonly caused; keep ice on the treated area until it subsides.
  • Damage to Deeper Structures: Nerves, blood vessels, and muscles may be damaged.
  • Unsatisfactory Result: Possible unacceptable visible deformities or asymmetry.
  • Allergic Reactions: Rare; tell your provider about any allergies.
  • Medication Reaction: Tell your provider about all medications.
  • Herpes Simplex Virus: Can trigger a non-active outbreak.
  • Pregnancy: Not recommended if pregnant, may become pregnant, or breastfeeding.
